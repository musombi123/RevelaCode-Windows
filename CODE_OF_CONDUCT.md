# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful, constructive environment for everyone who participates in this project, regardless of experience, background, identity, or point of view.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique code, decisions, and ideas; do not attack people.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact honestly.
- Give actionable feedback and accept feedback professionally.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property, and project security.
- Keep discussions focused on the project's purpose.
- Credit original work and disclose third-party assets, dependencies, and licences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, deliberate disruption, sexualised harassment, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Spam, malicious code, credential theft, and knowingly submitting unsafe or unlicensed material are also prohibited.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project. Decisions should be proportionate, documented where practical, and based on observable conduct.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub's private reporting/contact mechanisms. Do not post sensitive personal information or security details in public issues. Include relevant links, dates, and a concise account of what happened. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, project events, and other spaces where someone is representing or participating in the project.
